# The Squishy Comeback: animation script

A short interactive animation (about 45 seconds) made to cheer up someone whose two
dumpling squishies were broken during a therapy session at school.

**Characters:** Two glittery dumpling squishies, **Bao** (pink) and **Mochi** (purple).
They have kawaii faces, top pleats and sparkles inside, like the real toys.

**Tone:** soft, sweet and a little funny. The message is that nobody is to blame, and
that her work matters.

---

## Scene 0: The envelope (tap to open)
*Soft pink screen with a single button.*

> **Hey {NAME}, two little friends have something to tell you.**
> [ Open ]

## Scene 1: The accident (≈12s)
*The lights dim. Bao and Mochi drop in, but they look grey, cracked and bandaged.
They tremble and a tear rolls down each face.*

> CAPTION: "Once upon a time, two dumpling squishies lived a very squishy life."
> CAPTION: "Then one day, they got squeezed a liiittle too hard…"
> CAPTION: "They cracked. And they missed you already."

## Scene 2: The magic (≈6s)
*Pause. Then a warm glow rises behind them and sparkles swirl around. The bandages
pop off and float away. The cracks close, the colours and glitter come back, and the
frowns turn into smiles.*

> CAPTION: "But squishies know a secret…"
> CAPTION: "Love is the best glue. ✨"

## Scene 3: The happy bounce (≈4s)
*Bao and Mochi do three big squashy bounces, one after the other, like a little dance.*

## Scene 4: What they want to say (≈15s)
> CAPTION: "It wasn't anyone's fault. The kiddo just had some really big feelings."
> CAPTION: "You give kids a safe place to squeeze those feelings out. Sometimes that's a squishy."
> CAPTION: "We got squeezed that hard because we were doing our job. Just like you do yours, every day."

## Scene 5: The finale (interactive)
*A shower of hearts. The dumplings keep hopping on their own.*

> **We love you, {NAME}! 💗**
> Tap us. We're unbreakable now.
> *P.S. {YOUR PS LINE}*

*When she taps a dumpling it squishes like jelly and hearts pop out. A "Watch again"
button replays the story.*

---

## How to make her day, beyond the animation
1. Send her the link with no explanation, just "open this 🥟".
2. Buy two new dumpling squishies (the glitter "dumpling stress balls" in the photo)
   and hide them in her bag. The P.S. line in the finale sets that up.
3. Get a spare set for her therapy room, so the kids have their own and hers stay safe.
